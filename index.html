Privacy Policy

How Aurora Content Studio handles data when you run and operate the studio.

Last updated: May 27, 2026  ·  Effective: May 27, 2026

In short: Aurora is a self-hosted tool. Your generated media and operational data live in your own local asset vault and database — there is no Aurora-operated cloud. The only data that leaves your machine is the text Aurora sends to its AI providers (Google Gemini and Microsoft Edge TTS) to generate stories, narration, and scores.

01 About this policy

Aurora Content Studio ("Aurora", "we", "our") is an autonomous, UI-driven AI studio that plans, writes, narrates, subtitles, scores, renders, and stores short-form cinematic videos. Aurora is designed to run on infrastructure you control — your local machine or your own server (VPS) — not on servers operated by us.

This policy explains what data Aurora processes, where it goes, and how long it is kept. It applies to the Aurora software and dashboard. It is a plain-language, informational notice and is not legal advice.

02 Data we process

Aurora processes the following categories of data, all under your control as the operator:

CategoryExamplesWhere it lives
Creative inputs Master prompt, style fields, story ideas, character and theme settings you enter in the Prompt Studio Your PostgreSQL database; sent to Gemini to generate content
Generated assets Final videos (final.mp4), narration audio, subtitles, scripts, thumbnails, metadata Your local asset vault (storage/assets/…)
Operational data Post records, pipeline stage status, domain events, analytics scorecards, job queues Your PostgreSQL database and Redis instance
Account/auth data Operator login session (JWT) used to access the dashboard, if authentication is enabled Your server / your browser

Aurora does not collect personal information from end-viewers of your published videos, and it does not run advertising or third-party analytics trackers inside the studio.

03 Third-party AI services

To generate content, Aurora sends text to external AI providers. When it does, that text is processed under their privacy terms, not ours:

  • Google Gemini — receives prompts and creative text to plan stories, write scripts, suggest music moods, and produce analytics. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Microsoft Edge TTS — receives narration text to synthesize speech audio. Governed by Microsoft's Privacy Statement.
If GEMINI_API_KEY is left empty, Aurora runs in offline mode and returns deterministic mock content — in that mode no creative text leaves your machine. Similarly, if the Edge TTS endpoint is unreachable, placeholder audio is generated locally.

04 Storage & retention

Aurora uses local, self-managed storage only. There is no Aurora cloud, no Google Drive, and no Google Sheets. Generated media is written to your local asset vault, organized by year / month / week / post-id.

You decide how long data is kept. Assets remain in your vault until you delete them or until the studio's own cleanup routines remove them according to settings you configure. Removing a record from the dashboard or deleting the vault directory permanently removes the underlying data.

05 Cookies & sessions

The Aurora dashboard may use a session token (JWT) stored in your browser solely to keep you signed in. This is functional, first-party, and is not used for tracking or advertising. Aurora does not set third-party cookies.

06 Children & family content

Aurora produces family-friendly, emotionally-driven storytelling (themed around Milo the golden retriever). The studio is an operator tool intended for adults and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. It is your responsibility, as the operator, to ensure your published content complies with the rules of the platforms you distribute it on.

07 Security

Because Aurora is self-hosted, the security of your data depends on how you deploy it. We recommend keeping your .env secrets (API keys, JWT_SECRET, database passwords) private, serving the dashboard over HTTPS in production, and restricting network access to your database and Redis. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure.

08 This website

This privacy page is published via GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub may collect standard technical information such as your IP address, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. This page itself does not set cookies or run analytics.

09 Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Aurora evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of Aurora after an update means you accept the revised policy.
